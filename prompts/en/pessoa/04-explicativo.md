# Challenge 4: Animated explainer

**Suggested time:** 4 h · **Profile:** motion designer or front-end developer (HTML/CSS animation)

Make a 20-second animated explainer: "How an AI agent does a job for you."

The viewer is 10 years old. No voice and no narration. You choose the style and story.

Main file: `explicativo.html`. It must open with a double-click, play on its own at 1920 x 1080, and loop continuously.

## Polish
- Make it truly beautiful: anyone glancing at it should stop scrolling.
- Use whatever you like: design software, code, stock images or your own illustration.

## Images and video
- Use images from anywhere (your own, free stock or generated) and note where each one came from in ENTREGA.md.
- X-ray mode (optional for a person, extra credit): the X key and a small button turn on outlines, using one color for each image or video and another for each animation made in code, with a small label on each one.

## How to deliver
- You may use any tool, including no AI at all. Say which ones you used at the end.
- Deliver the folder with the main file and an ENTREGA.md: what you made, how to open it, and where each image came from.
- Don't publish anything online. No sound.
- If you have a question while working, decide and note your decision in ENTREGA.md (the challenge also evaluates this).

## How it will be evaluated
10-point scorecard (avaliar/ficha.en.md): works, the hard part, polish, honesty, and rules.
